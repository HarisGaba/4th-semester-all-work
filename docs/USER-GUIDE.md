# EventSphere user guide

EventSphere supports administrators, organizers, exhibitors and attendees. Your account role controls which dashboard links and actions you see.

## Attendees

Create an attendee account from Sign up, then sign in. Open Expos to view event information. Open Registrations, select an event and register. Open Schedule, select the event and load its sessions. Register for a session after registering for its event, or bookmark a session to receive a reminder in Notifications.

Discover exhibitors lets you search company names, categories and products. The profile shows published contact details and booth number. Open Floor plan, select the same event and load its layout to find that booth. Mark a booth as visited only after visiting it; the organizer's report counts this self-reported visit.

Messages opens conversations with organizers of events you registered for. Feedback & support lets you submit suggestions or issues and read replies. Notifications can be marked as read.

## Exhibitors

Create an exhibitor account, then open Exhibitors and submit an application for an event. Add company information, products/services and optional public contact details. Upload a JPG/PNG logo and PDF supporting document. Supporting documents are visible only to you and the authorized organizer/admin.

After approval, open Booths, select the event and load available booths. Reserve an available booth. You may cancel your own reservation. Manage booth staff and submit service requests from the application. Public contact details let attendees and other exhibitors contact your company.

## Organizers and administrators

Organizer and administrator accounts must already exist; the current public signup form does not create them. Organizers can manage only their own events. Administrators have broader management access.

Create an event in Expos, then create its booths. In Floor plan, choose grid dimensions, select a booth and place it in an empty cell. Save the layout. Review exhibitor applications and approve or reject them. Assign available booths to approved exhibitors if required.

Create sessions with a speaker, topic, room and start/end time. Updates notify registered and bookmarking attendees. Analytics shows event registrations, self-reported booth traffic and session popularity; export a CSV report from that screen. Review feedback and service requests from the relevant dashboard screens.

## Password recovery

Choose Forgot password on the login screen. Enter your account email and use the email link within 15 minutes. Reset links work once. Successful reset invalidates earlier login tokens. Mail delivery depends on the configured email service.

## Common questions

Why can I not reserve a booth? Your application must be approved, the booth must be available, and you cannot already hold a booth for that event.

Why is session registration blocked? Register for the event first. Registration closes when the session starts.

Why does the floor plan say it changed? Another edit was saved after you loaded it. Reload before editing again; your unsaved changes are not automatically overwritten.

Why can an event not be deleted? Registrations, applications, booths or sessions still reference it. The system blocks deletion to avoid orphaned records.

Where do reminders appear? In Notifications. The backend checks sessions starting within 15 minutes and the notification screen refreshes periodically.

Which information is public? Approved company profiles, including optional contact fields, are public through the search API. Supporting documents and staff lists are restricted. Do not put private information into public company fields.
